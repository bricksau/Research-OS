# 【白泽·辟邪】Anti-AI Shield: 消除机器痕迹的学术律令库

> **核心法则**：真实的人类学者用**严谨、克制且有呼吸感的语言**陈述事实，而 AI 则倾向于用**对称、浮夸、空洞且带有说教味的套话**填充篇幅。白泽·辟邪旨在彻底清除后者。

---

## 一、 AI 文本的三大典型病灶 (The Three AI Hallmarks)

1. **病灶一：虚假的高潮与廉价的赞美（Hyperbolic Flattery）**
   - AI 极度喜欢给普通事实贴金，堆砌 “profound”, “revolutionary”, “cornerstone” 等大词；
   - **学者思维**：事实本身有力量。把数字列出来（降幅 87.4%）、把机制写清楚，比写 10 个 “profound” 都更有说服力。

2. **病灶二：单调的句式钟摆（The Monotonous Metronome）**
   - AI 倾向于连续输出由 `, which ...` 或 `Not only ..., but also ...` 构成的 28~32 词复合句；
   - **学者思维**：短句定性，长句剖析。长短交错，方有学术张力。

3. **病灶三：机械的三段论排比（Mechanical Tripartite Form）**
   - AI 总是喜欢在段落末尾强行写一句总结：“In conclusion, these findings not only highlight X, but also illuminate Y, ultimately providing a comprehensive framework for Z.”
   - **学者思维**：事实说完了就干净利落收束，或者直接指出尚未解决的局限。

## 二、 词汇清洗黑名单与学者替代词库

### 1. 英文学术清洗表 (English Banned List & Alternatives)

| AI 常见禁用词 (Banned) | 审稿人印象 | 真实学者推荐替代方案 (BaiZe Approved) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **crucial / pivotal / vital** | 空泛夸大，像高中生作文 | `central to`, `accounts for a major share of`, `serves as a primary conduit` |
| **profound / revolutionary** | 耸人听闻，缺乏实证依据 | `substantial`, `measurable`, `structural`, `consequential` |
| **delve into / explore** | 典型的 ChatGPT 提示词回音 | `examine`, `analyze`, `trace`, `disaggregate`, `scrutinize` |
| **testament to / beacon** | 假大空文学修辞 | `evidence of`, `is consistent with`, `reflects` |
| **not merely X, but also Y** | 机械对称句式（AI 最爱） | 直接拆分为两句，或使用独立副词；避免机械并列 |
| **strictly speaking** | 啰嗦无用 | 直接陈述限定范围，如 `under HS 6-digit classification` |
| **definitively proves** | 因果冒进，极易招致退稿 | `provides descriptive support for`, `is compatible with`, `points to` |
| **seamlessly** | 虚假宣传，工程中从无无缝 | `in coordination with`, `aligned with` |
| **tapestry / landscape** | 文学化比喻，显得业余 | `configuration`, `structure`, `distribution`, `regime` |
| **game-changer** | 商业杂志口号 | `structural break`, `inflection point`, `institutional shift` |

## 三、 句式呼吸感重构法则 (Rhythm Reconstruction)

### ❌ 典型 AI 腔调（死板、冗长、单调）：
> *"The rapid escalation of bilateral trade tensions in 2022 not only profoundly affected the global lithium battery supply chain, but also created crucial challenges for South Korean manufacturers, which ultimately compelled policymakers to delve into comprehensive reshoring strategies."* (38 words, 结构臃肿，大词堆叠)

### ✅ 白泽重构法（严谨、干脆、微观针刺）：
> *"Bilateral trade tensions escalated sharply after 2022. This shift did not trigger an immediate rupture across the entire value chain. Instead, the pressure was concentrated in downstream battery cells, where South Korea’s export volume fell by 35% while upstream mineral dependence remained largely intact. Consequently, policy responses have focused on selective de-risking rather than outright decoupling."*
> - **长短句结构**：10词（定基调） $\to$ 14词（转折分化） $\to$ 26词（微观数据针刺） $\to$ 14词（收敛定性）。
> - **呼吸感**：读起来铿锵有力，有骨骼、有肌肉，绝对无法被任何 AI 检测器误判为机械生成。
